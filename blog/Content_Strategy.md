# Content Strategy: Dominating "Best IT Company in Kerala"

To rank for high-competition keywords like **"Best IT Company in Kerala"** or **"Software Development Kerala"**, we need to build topical authority through targeted, high-value content.

## 1. Targeted Keywords (High Intent)
- "Top 10 Software Companies in Kerala 2026"
- "Custom ERP Development for Small Business Kerala"
- "Mobile App Development Cost in Kerala"
- "Best IT Services for Wayanad Tourism Industry"
- "Flutter vs React Native: Kerala Developer Insights"

## 2. Proposed Blog Topics & Cluster Strategy

### Cluster A: Local Dominance (Wayanad)
- **Topic 1**: "Why Wayanad is Becoming the Next IT Hub of North Kerala"
  - *Goal*: Link the brand to the region's growth.
- **Topic 2**: "How IN-TA Solutions Scaled a Local Business in Mananthavady with Custom Software"
  - *Goal*: Proof of work and local success story.
- **Topic 3**: "5 Digital Trends Wayanad Entrepreneurs Must Adopt in 2026"

### Cluster B: State-Level Authority (Kerala)
- **Topic 4**: "Choosing the Best Software Development Partner in Kerala: A Checklist"
  - *Goal*: Capture "Best IT Company in Kerala" intent.
- **Topic 5**: "The Future of AI Integration for Kerala's SME Sector"
  - *Goal*: Position as thought leaders in modern tech.
- **Topic 6**: "Next.js vs Django: Why we chose this Stack for High-Performance Kerala Web Apps"
  - *Goal*: Showcase technical expertise (Task 3 alignment).

## 3. SEO Formatting Checklist for Every Post
- **Slug**: `/blog/best-it-company-kerala-checklist` (Optimized)
- **H1**: Must contain the exact primary keyword.
- **Internal Links**: Every post must link back to a relevant `/services/[slug]` page.
- **External Links**: Link to reputable Kerala tech news or government initiatives (e.g., Kerala Startup Mission).
- **CTA**: Every post should end with a "Discuss your Wayanad project" button.

## 4. Promotion Channels
- **LinkedIn**: Share snippets in Kerala Tech groups.
- **Google Business Profile**: Post these as "Updates" on your GMB profile to boost local signals.
- **Local Press**: Reach out to regional Wayanad news portals for guest features.
